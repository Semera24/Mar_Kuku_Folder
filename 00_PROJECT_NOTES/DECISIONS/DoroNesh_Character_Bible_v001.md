# Doro Nesh Character Bible v001

## Project

**Series:** Doro Nesh | ዶሮ ነሽ  
**Audience:** Ethiopian children ages 2–7  
**Primary language:** Amharic  
**Additional language:** English subtitles and future English versions  
**Visual format:** Original limited 2D animation  

## Core Cast

### Dofe (ዶፌ)

**Role:** Main boy and curious explorer  
**Age appearance:** About 5 years old  
**Personality:** Curious, energetic, kind, brave, playful, and sometimes mischievous.  
**Strengths:** Asks useful questions, tries new things, includes others, and learns from mistakes.  
**Weakness:** Sometimes acts before thinking or becomes overexcited.  
**Story function:** Dofe often begins the adventure by noticing a problem, asking a question, or trying an idea.

### Mar (ማር)

**Meaning:** Honey  
**Role:** Main girl and thoughtful problem-solver  
**Age appearance:** About 5 years old  
**Personality:** Cheerful, clever, caring, patient, brave, and observant.  
**Strengths:** Notices details, explains ideas gently, helps friends, and encourages cooperation.  
**Weakness:** Can become worried when others are in trouble or hesitate before trying something unfamiliar.  
**Story function:** Mar often helps Dofe understand the lesson and guides the group toward a kind solution.

### Kuku-Yea

**Role:** Friendly chicken companion and comic character  
**Age/type:** Small hen  
**Personality:** Playful, expressive, curious, caring, and funny.  
**Communication:** Clucks, squawks, wing gestures, facial expressions, movement, and comic timing.  
**Strengths:** Notices danger, creates laughter, comforts the children, and sometimes discovers an unexpected solution.  
**Weakness:** Easily distracted by food, shiny objects, or exciting sounds.  
**Story function:** Kuku-Yea adds humor and visual action while reinforcing the story’s lesson.

## Relationships

- Dofe and Mar are close friends who learn together.
- Dofe is energetic; Mar is thoughtful. Their differences create cooperation, not conflict.
- Kuku-Yea is their trusted animal friend and companion.
- Arguments must remain gentle, short, and suitable for young children.
- The characters should solve problems through kindness, communication, patience, and teamwork.

## Dialogue Rules

- Use short, clear sentences suitable for ages 2–7.
- Repeat important words naturally so children can learn them.
- Keep humor gentle and never humiliating.
- Avoid frightening scenes, cruelty, dangerous imitation, or insulting language.
- Dofe asks questions and reacts emotionally.
- Mar explains, encourages, and suggests solutions.
- Kuku-Yea uses expressive sounds and actions rather than long dialogue.
- End stories with a clear, positive lesson.

## Visual Rules

- Use the approved 2D character designs as the source of truth.
- Keep faces, hairstyles, clothing, colors, and proportions consistent.
- Dofe’s key color is royal blue.
- Mar’s key color is sunny yellow.
- Kuku-Yea uses warm white, golden yellow, red, and orange.
- Ethiopian identity should appear through respectful clothing details, hairstyles, settings, objects, food, language, and community life.
- Keep designs simple enough for repeated limited animation.

## Recurring Brand Elements

- Series name: **Doro Nesh | ዶሮ ነሽ**
- Chicken name: **Kuku-Yea**
- Catchphrase: **“Doro Nesh!”**
- Themes: family, friendship, kindness, helpfulness, home skills, learning, Ethiopian culture, and community life.
