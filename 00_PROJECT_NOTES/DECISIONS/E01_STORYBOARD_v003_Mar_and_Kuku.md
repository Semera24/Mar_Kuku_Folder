# EPISODE 001 — Storyboard v003

## Mar & Kuku's Big Counting Adventure — Learn 0 to 10

**Status:** Audio approved; ready for Palmier chapter build  
**Format:** 1920×1080, 30 fps, horizontal YouTube  
**Audience:** Ages 2–5, English-first, localization-ready

## Locked Identity

- Public channel: **Mar & Kuku**
- Episode lead: **Mar**
- Future boy friend: **Bubu**
- World: bright, calm Ethiopian-inspired learning village
- Keep all English in separate audio tracks; do not bake English words into artwork.

## Locked Animation Rules

1. Child understands the action with sound off.
2. Change a meaningful visual beat every 3–5 seconds in the hook and every 4–7 seconds afterward.
3. Use hard cuts between Mar poses. Inside a pose, only gentle purposeful keyframes.
4. No flashing, frantic cuts, loud surprises, or heavy effects.
5. Each new object appears one at a time and remains for the final count.
