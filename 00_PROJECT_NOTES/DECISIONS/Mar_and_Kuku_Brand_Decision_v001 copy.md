# Mar & Kuku — Brand Decision v001

**Status:** Approved before Episode 001 audio production  
**Date:** 2026-10-19

## Public Channel Identity

- **YouTube channel name:** Mar & Kuku
- **Lead character:** Mar
- **Chicken friend:** Kuku
- **Future boy friend:** Bubu
- **Creative world:** A bright, welcoming Ethiopian-inspired village learning world for children everywhere.

## Episode 001 Naming

- **Episode title:** Mar & Kuku's Big Counting Adventure — Learn 0 to 10
- **Opening lyric rule:** Say "Mar and Kuku," not "DoroNesh" or "Dofe."

## Production Rules

- Keep Mar's existing character design and pose library.
- Keep the existing village world, bright colours, and original educational style.
- Do not imitate another channel's characters, melodies, lyrics, branding, or shot sequence.
- Use simple visual storytelling, clear repeat gaps, and gentle purposeful movement.
- Keep the old `DOFE` asset folder temporarily; rename it to `BUBU` only after Episode 001 is complete.
