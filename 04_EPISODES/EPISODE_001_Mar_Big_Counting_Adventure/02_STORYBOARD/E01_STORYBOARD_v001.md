# EPISODE_001 — Storyboard v001

## Mar's Big Counting Adventure — Learn Numbers 0 to 10

**Format:** 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, horizontal YouTube episode  
**Target duration:** approximately 10 minutes 30 seconds  
**Audience:** preschool children, ages 2–5  
**Language:** English  
**Main character:** Mar  
**Editing rule:** hard cuts between different Mar PNG poses; gentle keyframed sways, nods, tiny steps, or claps within a pose. No fading between poses and no floating movement.

## Visual Rules

- Primary setting: `BG_Village_Open_Yard_v002.png`.
- Mar begins already in the village; no walk-in entrance.
- Keep Mar in the left or centre-left area when a large counting object is on the right.
- Use the full Number 0–10 character set in the main counting chapter.
- Background sheep remain small and quiet. Use Grazing normally; switch briefly to Head Up during the Number 10 celebration.
- Each item-counting chapter uses duplicates of one clean PNG prop. The objects appear one at a time, then remain visible for the final count.
- The final celebration overlay stays light and must not hide Mar, Number 10, or the chicks.
