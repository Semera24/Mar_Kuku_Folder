# EPISODE_001 — Mar’s Big Counting Adventure

## English Musical Script v002

**Original refrain used throughout:**

Count with Mar, come and see,  
Numbers are fun for you and me!  
Clap your hands and count along,  
Zero to ten in our happy song!

---

## 1. Hook and Welcome — 0:00–0:15

MAR:
Can you count all the way to ten?

[Show a quick, bright glimpse of Number 10.]

Come with me, little friends!

[SING REFRAIN]

---

## 2. First Count Together — 0:15–1:35

MAR:
Let’s count slowly!

Zero! Zero means none.
Can you say, “Zero”?

[Repeat pause.]

One! One little sun.

[Repeat pause.]

Two! Two happy shoes.

[Repeat pause.]

Three! Three little trees.

[Repeat pause.]

Four! Four flowers at the door.

[Repeat pause.]

Five! High five!

[Repeat pause.]

Six! Six little sticks.

[Repeat pause.]

Seven! Seven is heaven!

[Repeat pause.]

Eight! Eight feels great!

[Repeat pause.]

Nine! Nine is fine!

[Repeat pause.]

Ten! Ten little friends again!

[SING SHORT REFRAIN]
Zero to ten, we can do it!
Zero to ten, hooray!

---

## 3. Egg Basket — 1:35–2:45

MAR:
Look! Eggs in a basket.

One egg.
Two eggs.
Three eggs!

[SING]
One, two, three,
Count the eggs with me!

MAR:
Can you count again?

One, two, three!

Wonderful!

---

## 4. Flower Garden — 2:45–3:55

MAR:
Pretty flowers are growing.

One, two, three, four!

Four flowers!

Oh! One more flower!

One, two, three, four, five!

[SING]
Four and five,
Flowers come alive!

MAR:
Five flowers! Great counting!

---

## 5. Fruit Tree — 3:55–5:05

MAR:
Look up at the fruit tree!

Let’s count the fruit.

One, two, three, four, five, six!

Six fruits!

One more is hiding behind a leaf.

One, two, three, four, five, six, seven!

[SING]
Six and seven,
Fruit from our tree!

MAR:
Seven fruits! Well done!

---

## 6. Birds in the Sky — 5:05–6:15

MAR:
Can you hear the birds?

Let’s count them in the sky.

One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight!

Eight birds!

One more bird is flying over.

One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine!

[SING]
Eight and nine,
Birds fly high!

MAR:
Nine birds! You are doing beautifully!

---

## 7. Ten Little Chicks — 6:15–7:25

MAR:
Look! Little chicks are coming to play!

Let’s count very carefully.

One, two, three, four, five…
Six, seven, eight, nine…
TEN!

[SING]
Ten little chicks,
Peep, peep, peep!
Ten little chicks,
Hop, hop, hop!

MAR:
Ten! Our special big number!

Clap for Number Ten!

[Clap rhythm: clap, clap, clap.]

---

## 8. Number Detective — 7:25–8:45

MAR:
Now, let’s play Number Detective!

What number is this?

[Show 0. Pause.]

Yes! Zero!

What number is this?

[Show 3. Pause.]

Three! Great job!

What number is this?

[Show 6. Pause.]

Six! You found it!

What number is this?

[Show 8. Pause.]

Eight! Wonderful!

What number is this?

[Show 10. Longer pause.]

Ten! You are an excellent Number Detective!

---

## 9. Final Review and Number 10 Celebration — 8:45–10:30

MAR:
Let’s count one last time!

[SING SLOWLY]
Zero, one, two, three, four,
Five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten!

MAR:
You did it!

You counted from zero to ten!

[Number 10 grows large and clear.]

[SING FINAL REFRAIN]
Count with Mar, come and see,  
Numbers are fun for you and me!  
Clap your hands and count along,  
Zero to ten in our happy song!

MAR:
Number Ten celebration!

[Mar: surprise → hands up → Clap 01 → Clap 02 → Clap 01 → Cheer/Dance.
Chicks hop gently. Sheep lift heads. Hold final happy picture.]

MAR:
I am so proud of you!

Bye-bye, little friends!
See you on our next adventure!
