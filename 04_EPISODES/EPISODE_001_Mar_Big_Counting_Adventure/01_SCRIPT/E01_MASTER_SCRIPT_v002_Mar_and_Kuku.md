# EPISODE 001 — Master Script v002

## Mar & Kuku's Big Counting Adventure — Learn 0 to 10

**Format:** 1920×1080, 30 fps, English, approximately 10 minutes  
**On-screen lead:** Mar  
**Public series name:** Mar & Kuku  
**Future friend:** Bubu (not used in Episode 001)

## Script Rule

The approved songs are the spoken/sung script. Build visual storytelling around them; do not add extra narration that competes with the music. Mar reacts through pose changes, pointing, clapping, thinking, listening, and gentle purposeful motion.

## Chapter Script and Visual Intent

| Ch. | Story purpose | Audio line / interaction | Visual direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Welcome | Approved Welcome song: greet little friends; welcome them to Mar and Kuku's big counting adventure; invite them to count and play. | Mar is already in the village with the grazing sheep. Brief teaser pops: egg, flower, apple, bird, chick, then Number 10. |
| 02 | Learn 0–10 | Approved slow 0–10 song. Each number must be visible before its matching sung word. | Mar points, talks, raises hands, then cheers for Number 10. |
| 03 | Eggs 1–3 | Approved Eggs song. Children count eggs into a basket. | Basket remains fixed; one egg arrives at a time; all three remain for review. |
| 04 | Flowers 1–5 | Approved Flowers song. Children count flowers. | One flower grows at a time; final five form a clean row or gentle arc. |
| 05 | Apples 1–7 | Approved Apples song. Children count red apples. | Apples appear one at a time in a simple tree-like upper-right arrangement. |
| 06 | Birds 1–9 | Approved Birds song. Children count birds in the sky. | Birds drift into a loose sky arc and remain readable. |
| 07 | Ten chicks | Approved Ten Chicks song. This is the learning peak. | Ten chicks gather; big Number 10 appears; sheep looks up; Mar celebrates. |
| 08 | Number Detective | Find Number 3, then 7, then 10. Each question receives the approved long instrumental thinking gap before the answer. | Use the detective stage. Show 2–3 number choices; correct number pulses after each gap. |
| 09 | Review and goodbye | Approved review song counts Zero through Ten once, then celebrates and says goodbye. | Quick number sequence, then village finale with Number 10, ten chicks, sheep head-up, confetti edges, and Mar waving. |

## Character Direction

- Mar never stands rigidly: use a very small sway, nod, point, or controlled bounce inside an unchanged pose.
- Use hard cuts between different pose PNGs. Never use crossfades that create ghost images.
- Keep teaching objects on the right/centre-right and Mar on the left/centre-left.
- Kuku is part of the public brand and songs. Do not add a Kuku visual shot until the Kuku animation asset is ready.

## Finale Rule

At the final happy moment, show Number 10, all ten chicks, Mar's Cheer pose, sheep Head Up, and the celebration overlay. Keep the centre readable and hold the final smile before fading to black.
