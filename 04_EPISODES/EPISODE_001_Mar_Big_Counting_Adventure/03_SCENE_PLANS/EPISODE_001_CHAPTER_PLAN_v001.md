# EPISODE_001 — Mar’s Big Counting Adventure

## Approved Chapter Plan v001

**Learning goal:** Learn and repeat numbers 0–10.  
**Target duration:** 10 minutes 30 seconds.  
**Style:** A calm, colourful Ethiopian village learning adventure.  
**Main character:** Mar.  
**Background:** Boardless village yard with small grazing sheep in the distance.  
**Ending:** Number 10 is the joyful achievement and celebration.

| Chapter                   |       Time | Learning activity                                                              | Mar’s main action                        |
| ------------------------- | ---------: | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- |
| 1. Welcome                |  0:00–0:35 | Mar welcomes children and explains they will count from 0 to 10.               | Warm idle, wave, talk                    |
| 2. Count Together         |  0:35–1:55 | Slow introduction to 0, 1, 2 … 10. Children get repeat pauses.                 | Point, listen, happy                     |
| 3. Egg Basket             |  1:55–3:05 | Count 1, 2, 3 eggs.                                                            | Point, talk, clap                        |
| 4. Flower Garden          |  3:05–4:15 | Count 4 and 5 bright flowers.                                                  | Think, point, happy                      |
| 5. Fruit Tree             |  4:15–5:25 | Count 6 and 7 fruit.                                                           | Talk, point, listen                      |
| 6. Birds in the Sky       |  5:25–6:35 | Count 8 and 9 friendly birds.                                                  | Look up, surprise, happy                 |
| 7. Ten Little Chicks      |  6:35–7:45 | Count 10 little chicks in the village yard. Number 10 becomes large and clear. | Point, hands up, excited                 |
| 8. Number Detective Game  |  7:45–8:55 | Mar asks children to find selected numbers: 0, 3, 6, 8, and 10.                | Listen, think, point, clap               |
| 9. Review and Celebration | 8:55–10:30 | Quick 0–10 review, then Number 10 celebration and goodbye.                     | Surprise → hands up → clap → cheer/dance |

## Important Creative Rules

- Mar is already on screen at the beginning; no walking entrance.
- Use slow, clear teaching pauses.
- Mar has gentle natural movement, not constant floating.
- Sheep remain small background atmosphere; they do not compete with the lesson.
- Number 10 is the biggest, clearest, happiest number moment.
- Each new object group appears only when needed for its chapter.
- All pose changes are direct hard cuts unless specifically approved otherwise.
