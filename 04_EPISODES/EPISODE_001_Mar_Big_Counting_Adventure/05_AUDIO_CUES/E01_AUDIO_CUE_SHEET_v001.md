## Non-Negotiable Generation Rule

For every counting song:

- Sing or say **one object and one number at a time**.
- Then leave at least **two to three full seconds** of instrumental space so children can repeat it.
- Never rush consecutive counts, overlap numbers, add unscripted object lists, or sing extra numbers.
- Make the final number in that chapter feel gently special, but not loud or frantic.
- Use original music only; do not imitate an existing children's brand or song.
